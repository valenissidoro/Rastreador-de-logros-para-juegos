    Roadmap

    Stage 1: Play Through The Story & Enjoy The Views!

    You will need to complete the game once to unlock the Challenge Stages and Story Stages, so for now, you can play through the story and enjoy the game at your leisure. You can choose to collect all of the Ambers and Seed Husks if you want to, but you can always clean-up via Chapter Select later if you prefer.

    Your first playthrough of the game should take about 2 hours at a relaxed pace. However, this will depend on your playstyle. If you are collecting everything and watching the cutscenes, then the game may take longer. You can refer to the following trophies if you want to watch a chapter-by-chapter walkthrough of the game, which includes all of the Ambers and Seed Husks:
    •  Journey's Start - Chapter 1: An Ill Omen.
    •  Are You Okay? - Chapter 2: Fall From Grace and Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child.
    •  A World In Collapse - Chapter 4: Help Big Man! and Chapter 5: A Way Around.
    •  New Beginnings - Chapter 6: A Promise To A Seed.

    You will also have some miscellaneous trophies, which unlock special interactions between the protagonist and the Fell Child. These trophies are unlocked from breaking the Ambers. The trophies that are listed below may unlock in different locations to what locations are shown in the guide, but you should have all of these trophies unlocked before the end of Chapter 5: A Way Around during your initial Story Mode playthrough.
    •  Jubilation
    •  Dance of Joy
    •  High Five?
    •  High Five!
    •  High...Five?

    Once you have completed the Story Mode, and unlocked all of this stage's trophies, you can move onto Stage 2 for the collectibles clean-up (if applicable), You can refer to Stage 3 and complete the Story Stages if you want to complete the game again while it's fresh in your mind, or you can refer to Stage 4 and complete the Challenge Stages if you want to, but it is recommended to focus on them later.

    Stage 2: Amber & Seed Husk Clean-Up (Optional)

    This stage is only necessary if you missed any of the Ambers or Seed Husk collectibles during your initial Story Mode playthrough.

    There are a total of 124 Ambers and 6 Seed Husks, but you won't need to collect all of these collectibles in one playthrough, as the game will keep track of your progress between your initial playthrough and any Chapter Select clean-up that you do. If you are using Chapter Select for either collectibles type, you can go to the chapter that you need and play until you are done with the clean-up. However, you will not need to use Chapter 1: An Ill Omen in this stage, as there are no collectibles there.

    Furthermore, there is no kind of tracking for the Ambers or the Seed Husks, whether in-game or in the trophy list, so it is recommended for you to refer to the  ​All Amber and  ​Mourning trophies for all of the information you need, including screenshots of each collectible's location, so that you can view at a glance which locations look familiar to you or not. ​​​​​​The game does not feature any kind of map, which can make it feel more difficult to pinpoint each collectible, but the game is linear enough to not make this feel like too much of a chore.

    You can also refer to the story-related trophies for a full chapter-by-chapter walkthrough, which includes all 130 collectibles, as well as all of the trophies that are unlocked for performing actions between the protagonist and the Fell Child.

    Stage 3: Story Stages

    This stage is similar to ​Stage 1, as you will be playing through the game again, but this time, you will be timed, and it is recommended that you don't collect the Ambers or the Seed Husks here, so that you can save yourself some time. The Story Stages mode provides you with the opportunity to complete the Story Mode in the fastest time possible. This trophy may sound tougher than it is, but 90 minutes will be considered lenient to some players.

    The Story Stages mode does have online leaderboards, but you do not need to be connected to PlayStation Network or the internet to unlock this trophy. The leaderboards have no impact on the trophy, but you can compare your time to other players or friends.

    Y​ou can refer to the  ​Speedy Descent trophy​​​ if you need any additional information or tips, and you will find a video walkthrough, which covers the entire game in the Story Stages mode, as well as some potentially beneficial methods and shortcuts that can help you save some time during your speedrun playthrough.
    Speedy Descent
    Completed all story stages within 90 minutes.

    Stage 4: Challenge Stages

    This stage is similar to ​Stage 3, as you will be timed for both of the Challenge Stage trophies. There are only 2 Challenge Stages, but this mode allows you to complete a parkour-style run in higher-difficulty stages that require you to descend to the finish in a limited time-frame.

    These trophies will be considered the toughest part of the 100% for some players, but it depends on the individual player and their experience with similar games. The time limits here are not as lenient as the the Story Stages mode, but it is not impossible to beat these times.
    • To unlock the  Challenger 1 trophy, you will need to complete Challenge Stage 1 in under 12 minutes.
    • To unlock the  Challenger 2 trophy, you will need to complete Challenge Stage 2 in under 6 minutes

    The Challenge Stage mode does have online leaderboards, but you do not need to be connected to PlayStation Network or the internet to unlock these trophies. The leaderboards have no impact on the trophies, but you can compare your times to other players or friends.

    Y​ou can refer to the trophies linked above if you need any additional information or tips. You will also find a video walkthrough in each trophy, which you can refer to and use as a visual reference to complete both of the Challenge Stage courses, especially as they contain some potentially beneficial methods and shortcuts that can help you save some time during your runs.

    Welcome To Where The Seeds Fall

    This is the debut title from CygamesEdge, and while it is a short game, there is plenty to explore beyond the initial playthrough. After you have completed the Story Mode, you will have the Story Stages mode, which allows you to complete the game again, but with a timer. You will also have access to the Challenge Stages, which are shorter courses with a timer. There are trophies related to all aspects of the game's content, and the while the completion time and difficulty will vary for each player, you will experience more of a challenge from the Challenge Stages and the Story Stages, compared to the Story Mode. However, with practice and persistence, achieving the 100% completion is not impossible, and this guide will provide you with plenty of resources to help you along your journey down The World Tree!

    Additional Information 

    The game has a total of 130 collectibles, which consists of 124 Ambers and 6 Seed Husks. You can refer to the  ​All Amber trophy for all of the Amber locations, and you can refer to the  ​Mourning trophy for all of the Seed Husk locations.​​​​​​ If you prefer a video walkthrough, you can refer to the  ​Are You Okay?,  ​A World In Collapse and  ​New Beginnings trophies, as the videos within those trophies include all of the collectibles in the game.​​​​​​​​​

    In addition to the Story Mode, collectibles and miscellaneous trophies that are locked behind the collectibles, you will have access to the Story Stages, as well as the Challenge Stages after you have completed the first playthough. You can refer to the  ​Speedy Descent trophy for more information regarding the speedrun​​​, and you can refer to the  ​Challenger 1 and  ​Challenger 2 trophies for more information regarding the Challenge Stages. All of these trophies are time-limited, so it is also recommended to watch the video walkthroughs, as they will help you gain more of an understanding of each trophy from a visual perspective.

    Mourning

    The Seed Husks depict a Fell Child who has sadly passed on, and interacting with them will allow them to be buried and at peace. There are a total of 6 Seed Husks throughout the game.

    They can be found in the following chapters:
    • Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child -  Are You Okay?
    • Chapter 4: Help Big Man! -  A World In Collapse
    • Chapter 6: A Promise To A Seed -  New Beginnings

    If you happen to miss any Seed Husks during your initial playthrough, you can use Chapter Select to discover the remaining Fell Children. Once all 6 Seed Husks have been discovered and interacted with, the trophy will unlock! You can refer to the trophies that are linked above, as each chapter is accompanied by a video walkthrough, which you can use to help you locate all of the Seed Husks if you missed any along the way.

    Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child

    When you have reached the first Flower of The World Tree of the game during Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child, you will need to throw it to the platform below and land on it. Next, you will find yourself at a short wall climbing section. Once you have finished this section and landed back on the ground, you will want to go to the bottom of this open area. Once you have reached the bottom, you will see the Seed Husk at the base of the large tree trunk. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.


    During the latter half of Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child, you will be tasked to avoid the Blighted areas and find an alternative route. Shortly after this, you will come to an area that has two Flowers of The World Tree placed below. You will want to land on the one on the right, as it is closer to the Seed Husk. You will see a beige stone ledge in front of you, so head to the right from there after you land, and you will see the Seed Husk in the back of this small area, near the base of a tree trunk. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.



    During the latter half of Chapter 3: The Peculiar Child, just after collecting the previous Seed Husk location, you will come to one of the Blighted areas and you will need to make your way through it, towards the large, hollow tree on the left. Once you have made your way to the tree, you will see a small swarm of green fireflies in front of you, so safely make your way down, and you will see the Seed Husk on the left, against the tree trunk. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.



    Chapter 4: Help Big Man!

    After you have started Chapter 4: Help Big Man!, you will gain a new power, allowing you to reach higher places. To reach the Seed Husk that is in the starting area, you will want to go to the water, and make your way up to the higher platform in the background of this area. You can jump up to the higher platforms by pressing the  button, followed by the  button. Once you have made your way to the highest platform, you will see the Seed Husk on the right hand side. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.



    Near the end of Chapter 4: Help Big Man!, you will be diving into an enclosed body of water, and you will need to swim forwards, following the way-markers in front of you. You will be heading towards the current, which will pull you into the second pool. After you have landed in the second pool, you will see a shore area with an amber, as well as a walled area to the left. Once you have made your way to the walled area, you will crouch through the opening and the Seed Husk will be in front of you. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.



    Chapter 6: A Promise To A Seed

    When you have started Chapter 6: A Promise To A Seed, you will see a tall structure before the first amber of the chapter, with a platform on the left side that you can jump up to by pressing the  button, followed by the  button. Follow the path around to the right, and you will see the Seed Husk at the end of the path. To mourn the Fell Child, press the  button and it will be buried.

    Speedy Descent
    Completed all story stages within 90 minutes.
    15.38%
    Rare
    Time Limited Post Game
    You will not have access to the Story Stages until after you have completed the initial playthrough of the story, but when you do, you will see an informational message saying "you have unlocked Extras!", followed by "you can also attempt the story chapters again for a faster completion time", but there is no difference to the gameplay in Story Stages, as opposed to the initial playthrough, aside from the on-screen timer.

    As the game is linear for the most part, you shouldn't have too much trouble with navigating your way through the game again, especially if you are playing the Story Stages mode immediately after the initial playthrough. The 90 minute time limit is fairly lenient, especially as it is possible to complete a playthrough in under 1 hour. The author unlocked this trophy in 52 minutes, while skipping all of the cutscenes. The trophy will unlock after you have reached the end of the game, as opposed to after the credits, so this also saves you some time. 

    Story Stage Mode Tips

    • Skip the cutscenes by holding the  button, as you will save a lot of time over the course of the playthrough.
    • You can pause the game at any game, and the timer will be paused as well.
    • It is not recommended to combine this trophy with collecting the Ambers or the Seed Husks, but you can make progress towards those by using the Chapter Select option.

    You will not have access to the Challenge Stages until after you have completed the initial playthrough of the story, but when you do, you will see an informational message saying "you have unlocked Extras!", followed by "higher-difficulty Challenge Stages have been added to Extras". Despite the higher difficulty that these stages include, there are no difficulty settings or options within this game.

    The Challenge Stages will present more of a challenge for players, compared to the main game, but never fear! The Challenge Stages are not too difficult to complete if you keep practicing and learn some of the shortcuts or methods that you can utilize to save a couple of seconds during your successful run. In addition to this, you can refer to the Challenge Stage 1 Tips section below if you need some pointers, or you can refer to the video walkthrough that is linked below. It should be noted that it is possible to complete Challenge Stage 1 quicker than the walkthrough below, but there is some leeway to beating the 12 minute time limit if you make a mistake or two.


    Challenge Stage 1 Tips

    • Staying calm and focused can help a lot in completing this challenge, especially with so many platforms, ledges and obstacles, as well as sliding platforms to work your way through. However, the checkpoints are more frequent in this challenge compared to Challenge Stage 2, so this one might feel easier, despite the fact that it will take longer to complete.
    • Practice and improve. Regardless of your experience or skill level with platformers, it is possible to complete this challenge with practice, especially if you start the course off strong, such as the starting strategy in the beginning of the walkthrough below.
      • If you want to pause the Challenge Stage, you can press the  button, and the timer will pause as well.
    • Do not rely on the  button too much. If your character is running, this can make the surfaces feel slippery and there is a higher risk of falling or not gripping onto the next platform. This is especially true when you are on thin platforms or traversing between the swinging cage pendulums. Furthermore, if you are in a windy or stormy area, running can help or hinder you, so run responsibly.
    • It is important to watch the meter that is at the bottom of your screen, as you can only hover for a limited amount of time, until the meter refills, and you can only use the upwind gust once every time your character is airborne.
      • To refill your meter fully, you will need to be on a platform or using a hand ledge for around 2 seconds.
    • If you want to jump further, it is recommended to press or hold the  button to hover, and then you can use the  button to give yourself an upwards boost. This will help you go for further platforms, and this tip also works when you are descending, as you will want to flutter your way down and land gently to avoid injury or death to your character.
      • You can refer to the Helpful Information For Your Descent section for more details regarding the controls.
